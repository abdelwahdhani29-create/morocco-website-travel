About GoMoroccoAI

GoMoroccoAI is an independent Morocco travel guide founded and edited by Abdelwahd Hani. It helps visitors plan realistic trips with clear city guides, cultural context, practical checklists, and links to official information.

Abdelwahd combines first-hand familiarity with Morocco and independent research. Articles may use digital and AI-assisted tools during drafting, but every published page is reviewed, edited, and approved by Abdelwahd Hani. AI output is never treated as a source.

Time-sensitive claims about entry rules, safety, transport, currency, and tourism statistics are checked against official sources and labelled with a verification date. We correct material errors promptly; readers can send corrections directly to gomoroccoai@gmail.com.

Editorial policy

How we research

We favour official Moroccan authorities, transport operators, international organisations, and clearly identified primary sources. Commercial listings and prices are treated as examples, not guarantees.

Updates and corrections

Important guides show when they were last reviewed. If a rule or fact changes, we update the page and its verification date. Corrections can be emailed to gomoroccoai@gmail.com.

Independence

Advertising does not determine our conclusions. Sponsored or affiliate relationships, if introduced, will be labelled clearly near the relevant content.
