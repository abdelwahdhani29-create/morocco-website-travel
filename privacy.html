Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosures

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Consent & Information Collecting

GoMoroccoAI is an independent travel guide operated by Abdelwahd Hani. The site stores language, planner, and consent preferences in your browser. Optional Google Analytics and Google AdSense services load only after you choose “Accept” in the cookie banner.

2. Specific Cookie Usage Disclosures

Essential browser storage remembers your language, planner settings, and consent choice. If you reject optional cookies, Google Analytics and AdSense are not loaded. Clear this site's browser data to reset your choice.

Cookie Classifications we use:
  • Essential Preferences: Saves site direction state (LTR), selected language ('en'), and cookie acceptance preferences.
  • Local Cache: Temporarily remembers filtered cities search terms when browsing dashboards.

If advertising is enabled, we may use Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies only in accordance with the consent choices and applicable law.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the official Google Ads Settings page.

Google services are optional and activate only after affirmative consent. Google may then receive technical data such as IP address, browser information, pages viewed, and advertising identifiers under its own policies. A Google-certified consent platform must be configured before serving personalised ads to relevant European visitors.

4. Privacy Inquiries

For any privacy-related questions concerning local client-side memory safety, please contact us at gomoroccoai@gmail.com.

5. User Rights & Data Control

You can clear this site's localStorage and cookies through your browser. GoMoroccoAI does not ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal data through a form. Hosting and optional third-party providers may still process technical request data such as IP address and browser information under their own policies. Users can manage Google advertising choices at Google Ads Settings.

6. Data Retention

Planner and language preferences remain in local browser storage until cleared. Consent records remain until cleared. External providers such as Google, Vercel, Unsplash, and Pexels apply their own retention policies to requests they process.

7. Children's Privacy

The GoMoroccoAI website and travel guide platform are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use or provide any information on this site.
